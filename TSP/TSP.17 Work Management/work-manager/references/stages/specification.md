# Specification

Deciding what will be built: what it is, how we will know it is done, and what is out of scope, said out loud.

**What to expect:** one specification usually; a large piece of work may split it (a functional specification and a design, say), each a document of its own. For medium work the plan is a checklist at its end.

**Prompts:** what will be built? How will we know it is done: the acceptance criteria, each one checkable? What is out of scope? What does it depend on?

The acceptance criteria also go on the WBS row.

**Done enough when:** *could someone else build this from the spec, and check it?*
