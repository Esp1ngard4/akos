# Plan

Deciding how it will be built: the steps, their order, a rough size for each, and what could go wrong.

**What to expect:** one plan, agreed before building starts. A large piece of work may plan each part on its own.

**Prompts:** what are the steps, and in what order? How big is each, roughly? What could go wrong, and what would we do? Who agrees the plan?

**Done enough when:** *does the person approving it know what they are agreeing to?*
