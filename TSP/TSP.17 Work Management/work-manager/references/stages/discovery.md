# Discovery

Finding out before deciding: what is there today, the problem in the words of whoever has it, the evidence, and the options where there is more than one.

**What to expect:** as many documents as the finding out needs. A vendor survey, a round of interviews, a look at the current system and a comparison of options are each a document of their own (`… - Discovery - Interviews.md`), or one document for small questions.

**Prompts:** what is there today, and how does it work? What is the problem, and who has it? What is the evidence? What are the options, and what would each cost?

**Done enough when:** *could someone who was not there explain the problem, and why it is worth solving?*
