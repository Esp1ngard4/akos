# Implement

Doing the work, and keeping its trail: notes as you go, dated, with what was done, what was decided, and anything that changed the plan and why.

**What to expect:** notes, newest on top. Where the work produces documents of its own (a test report, a hand-over note), each is a document of its own beside them.

**Prompts:** what was done today? What was decided, and why? What changed from the plan? Before closing: each acceptance criterion, and its verdict (met, partly met and where the rest went, or not met).

**Done when:** *the verdicts are written, and the WBS row can close on them.*
