# Type module — deck skeleton

**Renders**: `<channel>-deck.md` — a slide-by-slide skeleton. **Never** a `.pptx`.

This module governs **rendering only**. The angle, Six Questions, outline and claims come from the shared `backbone.md`, and the claim check, anti-AI voice check, refinement loop and `writing-principles.md` all still apply unchanged.

## What you produce

```markdown
## Deck skeleton — <working title>

**Audience**: <from the plan, or as answered>
**The one thing they should leave with**: <a single sentence>

### 1. <Slide headline — the claim this slide makes>
**On the slide**: <the minimum that has to be visible>
**What I say**: <the point, in the author's voice>
**Evidence**: <the artifact, number or story that backs it — or "none yet">
```

Close with:

```markdown
**The ask**: <what the audience is being asked to decide, fund, or do — or "none, this is informational">
**Slides I cannot yet fill**: <every slide whose evidence line says "none yet">
```

## Rules

**A slide headline is a claim, not a topic.** "Intake automation" is a topic and tells the reader nothing. "Intake automation cut cycle time by half" is a claim, and it is what the slide has to earn.

**Every slide names its evidence, or admits it has none.** An unfilled evidence line is the most useful output this module produces — it is the list of what the author must go and get before presenting. Never quietly fill it with something plausible.

**One claim per slide.** A slide making two claims is two slides, or one of them is filler.

**The ask is explicit.** A deck that never states what it wants is a document, and the author should know that is what they have.

**Confidential material is the author's call, not yours.** Where a strategy lists topics to avoid — internal figures, named clients — those apply here exactly as they do to a public post. A deck being internal does not lift them; the author lifts them, deliberately, or they stay.

## What this must never do

- Write, generate, or reference a `.pptx`, or specify visual design, colours, layout, or animation.
- Invent a number, a logo, a customer name, or a quotation.
- Fill an evidence line to make the skeleton look complete.
- Add a "thank you" or "questions?" slide. They carry nothing.
